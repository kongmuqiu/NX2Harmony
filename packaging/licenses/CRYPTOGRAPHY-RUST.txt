cryptography 50.0.2: the Rust crates in cryptography/hazmat/bindings/_rust.pyd
=============================================================================

cryptography's Rust extension, _rust.pyd, is built from the crates below; the list and the licences are those of the SBOM
the cryptography 50.0.2 wheel carries (cryptography-50.0.2.dist-info/sboms/cryptography-rust.cyclonedx.json, shipped in
_internal). OpenSSL 4.0.3 is linked statically into _rust.pyd (sboms/sbom.json there), under the Apache License 2.0.

Where a crate offers a choice, NX2Harmony takes the Apache License 2.0; its text is cryptography's LICENSE.APACHE
(_internal/cryptography-50.0.2.dist-info/licenses/LICENSE.APACHE; also licenses/cryptography/LICENSE.APACHE here).
The crates that offer no Apache choice, or carry their own copyright line with it, follow with their licence files,
taken from the crate archives on crates.io (their sha256 equal the SBOM's).
